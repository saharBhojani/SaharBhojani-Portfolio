Exhibited

Merdeka Through the Lens

Three frames selected for Discover Malaysia — a photography competition and exhibition on independence, heritage and progress.

01

Towers of Time

Standing in a single frame, the historic clock tower and the futuristic skyscraper reveal how far Malaysia has progressed. The ornate, classical architecture reflects the nation's roots, while the sharp glass structure represents innovation, ambition and a modern future. Through layered composition, vertical lines and an upward perspective, the image captures Merdeka as a journey from heritage to progress without losing cultural identity.

02

Guardian of Tradition

Wearing traditional headwear with quiet confidence, the vendor stands as a guardian of Malaysia's cultural identity. His steady gaze suggests strength, dignity and pride, while the surrounding textiles add layers of colour, history and meaning. In the spirit of Merdeka, this portrait celebrates empowerment through heritage — the courage to honour one's roots and carry tradition forward with purpose.

03

The Sweetness of Malaysia

Colourful rows of kuih create a welcoming path toward the two young women, giving the scene warmth, depth and a sense of abundance. Every flavour carries a memory, every shade feels familiar, and every shared moment becomes part of something larger. Beneath the softness is the quiet strength of culture and belonging. This is Merdeka felt not in monuments, but in the sweetness of home.
