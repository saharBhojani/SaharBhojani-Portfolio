Kuantan
City

A type system for a coastal city, and the campaign site it had to survive.

Set in a typography class: build a brand guideline for a Malaysian city, then prove it by running a full campaign through it. The guideline came first — palette, seven-level type scale, body sizes — and the website was the test. If the system couldn't carry a tourism site, it wasn't a system.

01

The guideline came first

Seven heading levels and three body sizes, locked as Figma text styles before layout began — H1 at 188pt down to 12pt small copy. The palette took its cues from the place itself: sage and indigo from the water, terracotta and mustard from the roofs and the food.

02

Wireframe, then colour

The sequence was fixed early: hero, places to visit, an itinerary drawn on a real map, gallery, best time to visit, then food. The itinerary section was the risky one — a route through five stops with numbered cards along a dashed path — so it was wireframed before any photography arrived to flatter it.

03

The finished site

The whole page, uncropped. Every heading is a level from the guideline and every block of colour is a named style — nothing on this page was chosen ad hoc.
