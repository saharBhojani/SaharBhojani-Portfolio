Unsent

Confess in colours — an exhibition for the things you never said out loud.

My DGM final year project — a team exhibition, not a website exercise. Visitors came to our booth, bought a confession card or simply wrote a message to someone, pinned it to the interactive wall, and walked away having finally said it. My contribution was the digital half: the website that gave the show a home, a shop and a voice.

01

The concept came from my team lead

Credit where it belongs: the premise was my team leader's idea — that everyone carries something they could not say, and that a stranger's booth is a safer place to say it than the person's inbox. My job was to translate that premise into digital form and hold it steady across every page.

02

Not classy — fun and playful

This was a deliberate choice against my own defaults. A confession site could easily go quiet, tasteful and dim — but the exhibition was balloons, stickers, a prompt wheel and candy. Heavy feelings needed a light room. So the site went the other way: a wobbly multicolour logotype, scalloped edges, watercolour washes, and a product card in a different saturated colour every time.

{{ m.title }} {{ m.body }}
03

The pages

Five templates carrying one voice: home, about, submit, shop and terms. Even the terms page keeps the wobbly logotype and scalloped edge — a legal page in the same handwriting is part of the argument that this brand means it.

04

The show, live

The booth ran in the mall atrium: an interactive wall of pinned envelopes, a five-step tutorial board, a prompt wheel for anyone who froze, printed merch, and a screen carrying the brand. Strangers stopped, wrote, pinned, and left lighter — the whole point of the thing.
