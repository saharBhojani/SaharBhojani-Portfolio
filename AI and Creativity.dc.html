The premise

Where does the author sit?

The generator here is deliberately dumb — a seeded rule set choosing between five layouts, four palettes, two typefaces and a handful of words. It has no taste. Every composition it produces is technically valid, and most of them are forgettable.

Which is the point. Generation is cheap; the scarce act is refusal. When you press sign this one you take responsibility for a composition you did not draw — and that decision is the design work, exactly as it is when the tool is a model instead of a rule set.

I built this instead of writing an essay because the argument only lands once you feel yourself rejecting things.
