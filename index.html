02 — About

About me

Sahar — Malaysia

I design in the gap between craft and system — where a typeface choice and an information architecture are the same decision.

Studying Graphics & Multimedia at Sunway University on the Dean's List. I lead the media team for Sunway's international student ambassadors and assist in the design lab, teaching peers Figma and design fundamentals.

My work moves between interface, identity and experiment — a redesign grounded in stakeholder interviews one week, a generative type study the next. I care most about clarity: say the thing plainly, then make it beautiful.
